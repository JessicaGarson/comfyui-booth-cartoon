The full prompt used for the booth cartoon, pasted into the MiniMax H3 node. It replaces the template's gaming-mouse prompt.

```text
Create a playful 2D cartoon animation based on the attached reference, Picture 1.
Visual style: Bright, hand-drawn animated-series style with bold, clean outlines, flat cel-shaded colors, friendly exaggerated expressions, and gentle, bouncy movement.
Scene: Recreate the tech conference booth from Picture 1. Preserve the booth layout, people, clothing colors, purple-and-white counter, backdrop banner, and existing runpod branding. Keep the focus on people interacting at the booth. No computer mice or product close-ups. Keep the runpod lettering stable and readable.
Dialogue and speech bubbles: All spoken dialogue must be in English, using only the exact lines below. Use comic-style speech bubbles with clear, readable English text that exactly matches the spoken dialogue. Each bubble must point toward the person speaking, appear when their line begins, and disappear when it ends. Show only one bubble at a time. No extra dialogue, invented words, subtitles, captions, or decorative text.
SHOT 1 — Establish the scene
Open with the same composition as Picture 1, redrawn as a cartoon. Slowly push the camera toward the booth. A smiling booth staff member gestures toward the booth while the attendees nod and lean in.
Booth staff says warmly: “Welcome to Runpod!”
Matching speech bubble: “Welcome to Runpod!”
Use a few small, text-free sparkles to emphasize the welcoming gesture.
SHOT 2 — Friendly response
Cut to a medium shot of the staff member and the attendee wearing a cap. The staff member gestures toward the banner. The attendee smiles and gives a cartoon thumbs-up.
Attendee in the cap says enthusiastically: “Great to be here!”
Matching speech bubble: “Great to be here!”
Add one soft pop sound to the thumbs-up. Keep the logo unchanged.
SHOT 3 — Cheerful ending
Pull back to a wide view of the whole booth. A few attendees stroll past while the booth staff and visitors wave and smile. End on a cheerful freeze-frame with the runpod booth clearly visible. No dialogue or speech bubbles in this final shot.
Audio: Upbeat, bouncy instrumental cartoon music at low volume. Clear English voices in the foreground, with natural delivery and lip movements matching the dialogue. Quiet, indistinct conference ambience without recognizable words. Sparse, gentle pop and boing effects timed to gestures. Lower the music during each spoken line.
```
